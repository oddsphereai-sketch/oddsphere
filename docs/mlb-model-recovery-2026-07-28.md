## Cap-free Home Run Lean path

The old five-play sleeve was profitable in aggregate but was a fixed cap. The
r13 top-15% rule varied in count but still imposed a relative quota. Neither
meets the no-cap requirement.

An absolute EV rule alone failed the untouched period. The accepted r14 rule
instead promotes every candidate that clears:

- projection, recent-survival, market-probability, and price-integrity floors;
- +200 through +650 odds;
- at least 12% expected value;
- a fixed 1.25 within-slate expected-value z-score.

The z-score normalizes a changing slate-wide price scale. It does not request a
minimum, maximum, or percentage of plays. Any number may clear it.

| Period | Bets | Record | Units | ROI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Discovery | 110 | 24-86 | +38.50 | +35.0% |
| Calibration | 58 | 10-48 | +9.55 | +16.5% |
| Validation 1 | 46 | 9-37 | +6.00 | +13.0% |
| Validation 2 | 39 | 8-31 | +13.35 | +34.2% |
| Untouched validation | 52 | 9-43 | +5.30 | +10.2% |
| Combined | 305 | 60-245 | +72.70 | +23.8% |

Daily counts ranged from 1 to 13 and averaged 6.5 on active dates. The
date-cluster ROI interval was -3.0% to +51.0%, with 95.7% of resamples
positive. Nearby lower z-score thresholds were also profitable in development
and untouched validation. The uncertainty still supports Lean, not Best Angle.

Compared with the old five-play sleeve, this path retained 169 historical
actions, promoted 136, demoted 71, and added 65 net actions.

actionable board by 32 while preserving 113 Leans and increasing transparent
Watchlists. This is a visible correction, not a hidden flat-board side effect.
