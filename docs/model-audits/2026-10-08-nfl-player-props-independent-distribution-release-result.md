## Decision

Advance the independently qualified Passing Attempts head. Do not advance Receptions or Receiving
Yards. The released point forecast uses no prop line, price, sportsbook, market probability, or
market movement. Target-excluded prices remain downstream benchmark, exact-price economics, and
movement evidence; they do not rewrite the released independent point forecast.

Passing Attempts forecasts team pass volume and the expected lead passer's share. The scorer
executes it through the existing NFL player-props board and sole leased writer; it adds no writer,
cron, provider call, or database loop.

## Probability selection

The original Weeks 1–9 / Weeks 10–18 selector did not produce a Passing Attempts candidate that
beat the actual released foundation distribution on the opened 2026 replay. That negative result
was preserved. Before the follow-up search, the robustness amendment froze four expanding-window
out-of-fold blocks: Weeks 7–9, 10–12, 13–15, and 16–18. A candidate had to improve aggregate Brier
and log loss without losing direction, improve Brier in at least three blocks, and avoid a block
regression greater than 0.005.

- Passing Attempts selected a 50/50 mixture of the released foundation distribution and a
  role-conditioned, mean-tertile empirical challenger. Robust out-of-fold Brier improved
  0.27087→0.25855, log loss 0.75152→0.71240, and direction 50.17%→51.55%.
- Receptions selected a 25/75 foundation/negative-binomial mixture followed by frozen logistic
  calibration. Robust out-of-fold Brier improved 0.25102→0.24263, log loss
  0.70096→0.67832, and direction 56.61%→58.01%. Its full production implementation nevertheless
  failed the mandatory current-board impact gate: six actionable Receptions became zero and no new
  actionable Receptions were promoted. The research result is retained, but none of that head is
  included in this production artifact or runtime path.
- Receiving Yards passed the historical selector but failed the explicitly opened 2026 probability
  and direction gates. Its point improvement remains research evidence only.

The 2025 opening archive covered 272/272 games, 32,999 paired book offers, and 14,283 unique
player/market thresholds. Those lines were evaluation thresholds only. Pushes were excluded, and no
price, line, bookmaker, movement, spread, or total entered either point model.

## Production contract

The portable expected-role artifact is generated by
`scripts/operator/export_nfl_player_props_expected_role_release.py`. Its shipped payload contains
only the Passing Attempts budget/share models, conditional probability release, team/opponent
state, and passer-relevant player state. Posted-prop presence may mark the named player active, but
the line and price are not consumed. Passing Attempts bypasses the preceding 90% market-implied
quarterback point head. The target-excluded market is kept as comparison/economics and movement can
still alter downstream grade thresholds; it cannot silently replace the independent forecast.

The release does not change Receptions, Receiving Yards, Passing Completions, Passing Yards, Rushing
Attempts, Rushing Yards, or Anytime Touchdown. It preserves the October 7 settlement-aligned
Rushing Attempts head and all prior market-direction arbitration outside Passing Attempts. Existing
locked member snapshots retain exact stored projection, probability, side, grade, price, stake,
evidence, and release precedence.

## Frozen current-board impact

The required apples-to-apples replay compares this candidate with the immediately preceding October
7 runtime on the same frozen Week 5 snapshot, forward evidence, current-season state cutoff,
retained market evidence, and 760 matched decisions. Only Passing Attempts changes: all 34 Passing
Attempts projections and probabilities change, while every projection and probability in the other
seven markets is byte-identical. Passing Attempts changes two non-actionable grades (one Watchlist
to No Play and one No Play to Watchlist). The complete board remains nine actionable rows with zero
actionable promotions and zero actionable demotions; Passing Attempts remains zero actionable rows.
The pruned production artifact reproduces the pre-pruning candidate exactly across projection,
probability, grade, expected value, and market-economics fields.
