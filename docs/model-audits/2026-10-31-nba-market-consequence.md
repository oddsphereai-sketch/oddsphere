## Decision architecture

The independent score is an input, not a veto or a fixed blend partner. Each
complete same-provider/book/event/run/clock quote pair is de-vigged. Number
and price are compared through the released residual CDF. A same-input epoch
retains one exact opening anchor per book/provider/market in existing unlocked
writer captures. Relative quoted odds innovations transport the independent
contract probability; no2/5-point gates, 3-of-4 vote, forced0.25 crossing or
fixed market/model weight grants forecast authority. A first observation is
only an anchor. Repeated evaluations rebuild pristine independent scores.

An effective independent-law/release change explicitly retires the old epoch.
Unconsumed injury/status/formatting changes do not reset it. This conservative
policy does not establish a causal claim that all retired news was consumed.
Provider source time and receipt time are separate. Regressed/contradictory,
future-captured, malformed or cross-provider pairs cannot affect scores OR
grade-only pressure/follower consumers. A freshly collected genuinely unchanged
quote can be held-line evidence even when its book last-update is unchanged.

Same-book ML and Spread constrain one margin location and scale, not two votes.
Incompatible ordering is explicit conflict. Near-unidentified scale (within the
released CDF's numerical uncertainty) retains usable ML scalar evidence at the
released scale with a reason. Across books, probabilities at common ML/Spread
thresholds are pooled by median, then one coherent law is solved. Independently
averaging location/scale is forbidden: it can invent conviction. The independent
head and residual FAMILY remain, but conditional dispersion may change; this
is a new model assumption, not an unchanged calibrated variance claim.
Total evidence updates the separate Total coordinate. Scores, probabilities,
directions, exact-line value and grades consume the resulting single law.

## Signal consequences

| Evidence | Actual consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Direct ML, small/fixed-number price movement | Continuous margin/probability adjustment; natural confirmation, weakening or flip |
| Spread/Total number plus juice | Net economic change, including partial/full/over-compensation, not raw line direction |
| ML/Spread book disagreement | Joint constraints or explicit conflict, never duplicate votes or automatic favorite following |
| Intermediate path/partial buyback | Net update plus recent retracement conviction context; full buyback cancels old movement |
| Tickets/handle | Majority and money-minus-tickets distinguished; Away/Under normalized, source/provider retained |
| Held price/reverse reaction | Exact quote at/before split, then later quote reaction; tempers conviction, does not invent score points |
| Named lead/retail follow | Timed sequence context, zero extra forecast votes; rejected follow-through can temper Best |
| Missing splits | Does not erase valid price evidence; price-family failure does not fabricate a split-driven score |
| Target execution quote | Fixed-point final-book exclusion, not both sides' books; cycles explicitly retire coupled coordinates |
| Model/news refresh | Effective-law epoch retirement; unchanged forecast/status metadata does not erase market evidence |

The legacy categorical reader remains diagnostic, not the continuous head's
activation permission. Posture has no second score authority: it explicitly
reports the already applied score consequence. Opposing/uncertain/held-pressure
posture can temper Best to actionable Lean. Positive-price-EV and data quality
rules remain; grades use actual final probabilities rather than fixed correction
confidence. Unsupported inputs remain explicit, not called sharp confirmation.
Prediction exchanges are not sportsbook quotes and have no numeric authority
in this repair; their existing display/evidence paths remain separate.
