# CFB v1 frozen-calibrator grade policy predeclaration

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: frozen before 2024/2025 repeated confirmation

The qualified independent joint score PMF and the second tournament's qualified
Moneyline lane remain preserved. The second tournament is not a complete CFB
grade release because season-refitted Spread and Total calibrators produced no
confirmation actions at the 2023-selected thresholds.

This experiment tests a production-reproducible alternative: each disclosed
calibration family is fitted on 2022 forecasts and results only, before 2023
policy selection, and its coefficients are frozen unchanged through 2023,
2024, and 2025. The policy grid, bounded abstentions, minimum action counts,
weekly-cluster bootstrap, largest-win independence, Lean gate, and stronger
Best Angle gate are unchanged from the market-calibrated predeclaration.

This is materially different from annual refitting because forward decisions
will use one immutable calibration release rather than a threshold applied to a
moving probability transform. Confirmation seasons cannot influence either
calibration coefficients or policy thresholds.

The historical price limitation is unchanged: Spread/Total use -110 and
Moneyline uses the past-only spread-to-win curve with a synthetic 4.5% overround.
No historical CLV claim is permitted. Forward decisions still require real
named-book exact prices and target-excluded same-line consensus.

No quotas, forced actions, weekly caps, stakes, or reader-side grade overrides
are allowed. The independent PMF remains separately visible and is not
relabeled as the market-calibrated Bet probability.
