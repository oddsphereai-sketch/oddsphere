# Daily Edge grade / execution gate inventory

Status: complete production-path inventory; correction remains sport-scoped and shadow-first.

## What is legitimately rigid

Game/team/market identity, coherent two-sided probability construction, quote freshness, quote
provenance, pregame timing, immutable T-60 locks, model-release coherence, and required health data
remain fail-closed. Those conditions answer whether an evaluation is real. They are not confidence
inputs and must never be softened into a score.

## What is incorrectly rigid

Forecast confidence must not depend on one named book crossing an EV or American-price boundary.
Ordinary sharp, public, line, and price movement should contribute signed, bounded evidence to one
continuous score. They may affirm, resist, or—when their combined effect crosses the model's own
side probability—change a future unlocked prediction, but a single ordinary source is not an
automatic veto or flip. Only the final display tiers require category boundaries. Unlocked display
hysteresis should prevent a sub-point nudge from repeatedly changing the label.

## Shared contract added in shadow

`daily_edge_confidence_execution_contract_2026_09_04_r1` defines three invariants for new
sport-specific candidates: confidence grades read only a continuous score; optional hysteresis
stabilizes an unlocked label; and the exact quote resolves independently to `bet`, `shop`, or
`unavailable`. The contract is not wired into any production writer and cannot alter existing
locks, stakes, tracking, or member labels by itself.

The cross-sport contract is semantic and shared, but model releases, calibration, board replays,
and promotion decisions remain sport-specific. No model with an empty current board is silently
promoted from this inventory.
