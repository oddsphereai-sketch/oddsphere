## Predeclared production rule

The weekly CFB writer must not let one legitimately unpriced scheduled game suppress every other coherent game. When no canonical market anchor exists after the bounded BALLDONTLIE, SharpAPI, and Playbook reads, the writer will:

- preserve the game in the complete weekly wave;
- publish its immutable independent-football PMF as the only game projection;
- hold Moneyline, Spread, and Total exact-price Bet grades with `authoritative_market_anchor_unavailable`;
- create no evaluated tuple, stake, lock, or tracking row for that game;
- retain strict provider identity rules and never guess an ambiguous Sharp event.
