## What a signal can legitimately do to scores

For basketball/football, let M=home−away and T=home+away:

- A margin update dM at fixed Total changes home by+dM/2 and away by−dM/2.
- A Total update dT at fixed margin changes BOTH team means by+dT/2.
- A joint update changes home by(dT+dM)/2 and away by(dT−dM)/2.
- Dispersion/tail/dependence changes can alter win/cover probabilities without
  changing mean scores. Do not force fake score points to show activity.
- Soccer/hockey/baseball need their own score PMF/rate reconciliation; the
  basketball algebra is not a transferable scoring model.
- Flow without a quoted response may change conviction/grade but does not
  identify an exact number of points. A side can remain while confidence falls.
- Flips occur when the reconciled forecast naturally crosses the relevant
  win/cover/Total boundary, not because an action label demands a crossing.
- "Confirm" can mean no numerical change with explicit supportive evidence.
  Retain can be correct, but a dead/unimplemented score path is NOT retain.

Legend in the catalog: M=margin/result coordinate; T=Total coordinate;
J=joint score distribution; V=dispersion/shape; C=conviction/explanation;
E=exact-price economics; R=retain/reject with an explicit reason.
These specify eligible consequences, not compulsory directions or fixed points.
No observation proves the identity or eventual correctness of sharp bettors.
