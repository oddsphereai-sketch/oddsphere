## Common safety and product contract

Every sport retains one authoritative writer, target-book exclusion where
applicable, exact named-book price economics, immutable T-60 locks, and one
coherent final projection. Market evidence can promote, demote, or reverse a
prediction only inside that sport's validated lane. It cannot silently replace
the independent model, manufacture evidence from the evaluated sportsbook, or
rewrite a locked public record.
