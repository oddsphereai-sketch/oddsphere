## Replacement code and real source progress

`nbaHistoricalGamebook.ts` plus an offline normalizer now decodes original NBA
final boxes from retained PDF bytes, rather than treating a 2026 API receipt as
original 2023 knowledge. It verifies native source path/date/orientation, both
printed team headings, raw SHA, embedded creation, HTTP modification, actual
receipt time, final-score arithmetic, all player/team counts, exact seconds,
overtime duration and five starters. Original prior DNP designations are kept;
none supplies the target game's roster, actual participants or future availability.

Neutral-site reports omit VISITOR/HOME labels. Exactly two recognized team/record
headings must match the source URL's oriented pair. Repeated jersey numbers remain
explicit source warnings, not native player IDs or an automatic name collision.
Duplicate names and incoherent totals fail. Target outcomes and post-cutoff
publication are excluded by the explicit prior-book admission function.

Real replay normalizes 2,460 of 2,462 source games. Two reports have a one-second
embedded-creation/HTTP-modification inversion and stay unavailable. Separately,
307 normalized files have publication/regeneration clocks more than two days
after tip. Some January/February documents were regenerated in July 2025. Their
old URL dates cannot backdate those editions. Each actual later source clock is
retained and cannot populate an earlier cutoff.

The first page establishes box counts and their contribution numerator; it does
not establish each player's on-court advanced-stat context. Full-game contribution
share is explicitly **not** provider player PIE. For example, the original
Michael Porter Jr. box yields 18/217, whereas the captured paid PIE is 0.120. That
does not establish a paid-provider bug. The initial unpublished normalizer
prototype mislabeled full-game share as PIE; its original output is preserved,
marked invalid for fitting, and superseded by source R2 with separate field names.
No historical fit or live consumer used it. Net/PIE remain unqualified until their
actual on-court source calculation is verified; there is no zero-impact fallback.

`nbaHistoricalIdentity.ts` adds raw-body/query/native-row-bound official
CommonPlayerInfo decoding. Only stable names, college, country and draft facts
escape; team, active status and performance never do. This recovers native facts
for never-played members missing from the statistics index. Six real biographies
pass. Missing facts on the other provider are not silently filled by name.
