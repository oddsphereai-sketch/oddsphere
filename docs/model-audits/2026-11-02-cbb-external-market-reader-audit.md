## Reproduced release blockers

1. Static sign-triggered source-center replacement has a finite jump for an
   epsilon change in the independent margin, and checks the independent sign
   before relative transport. A nearly neutral independent away forecast plus
   a positive relative episode can therefore end HOME against both current
   source contracts, while a nearly identical independent home input becomes
   AWAY. This repeats the rejected NBA absolute-replacement failure.
2. Same-book ML/Spread sign agreement does not establish one current source
   distribution or independent/market error model. A fair ML.80 and Spread-3
   fair.50 is accepted while the substituted law yields ML about.59. Source
   prices do not supply the covariance or epistemic reliability needed to
   infer how to combine them with an independent forecast.
3. Selecting Circa's+1 Total change over Pinnacle's+10 solely by source name
   is not redundant-source deduplication or observed leadership. Different
   amplitudes remain unresolved competing evidence without further authority.
4. Original-to-latest endpoints erase a buyback: Circa spread prices
   -110/-110 -> -170/+145 -> -115/-105, with fresh opposing money/tickets
   during the reversal, produce the same receipt as the direct endpoint path.
   The new adverse reaction disappears and Best Angle survives.
5. Source flow is discarded when its own valid threshold differs from the
   executable target or when its source line changes. The source's own paired
   state and subsequent economic reaction must be retained; target economics
   are evaluated separately. Off-target pressure is not exact-target betting
   action or an identified bettor.
6. A BDL gap-fill anywhere advances global decision time; previously received
   splits then receive that later stamp, while original Owls quotes retain
   their earlier receipt. This can disable otherwise valid flow globally.
7. Current professional selected-side probability above.50 is called sharp
   confirmation even when the ongoing price episode opposes the pick and
   professional implied EV at the actual target is negative. Yale-6.5,
   Mount St. Mary's+26.5 and Le Moyne Under152.5 reproduce resistance alongside
   `sharpStatus=confirm`, `marketSignal=market_confirmed`, `best_signal` and
   the visible “Model + sharps agree” attribution.
8. The339-market diagnostic passes no flow inputs; its all-unavailable pressure
   count cannot establish actual production split-path coverage. The retained
   selected raw sources predate its anchors and do not qualify anyway.

82 remaining actionable rows have every available professional fair
probability below executable break-even. This is not82 proof-positive sharp
bets against the model: ordinary vig can make reference-implied target EV
negative. It requires separate model EV, professional price direction,
professional target-value corroboration and bound observed-flow evidence.
Do not invent a blanket negative-reference-EV veto to flatten the whole board.
