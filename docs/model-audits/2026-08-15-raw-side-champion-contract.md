# Raw selected-side champion contract — 2026-08-15

## Objective

Improve the outcome accuracy of the upstream published prediction before
optimizing grades or actionability. A calibration-only improvement that keeps
the same selected side does not satisfy this phase. Perfection is not required;
the release target is the largest chronologically supported improvement over
the current production side selector.

Oddsphere does not use prospective forward shadowing as a release gate. This
phase uses immutable locked historical records, chronological validation and
latest partitions, rolling-origin replay, exact locked-price board comparison,
a current-slate dry run, immutable release identifiers, and an explicit
rollback.

## Raw side-selection gate

Every candidate is fitted only on dates earlier than the dates it predicts.
The candidate probability is interpreted as the probability of the incumbent
selected side. When it is below 50%, the candidate selects the independently
qualified opposite outcome and publishes `1 - p`; otherwise it retains the
incumbent side and publishes `p`.

A raw side challenger qualifies through the standard accuracy path when all of
the following hold on common rows:

- validation accuracy does not regress;
- latest-partition accuracy improves;
- combined validation-plus-latest accuracy improves by at least 1 percentage
  point;
- the candidate actually changes sides in both validation and latest data;
- accuracy improves in at least half of the four rolling-origin folds;
- validation and latest Brier score regress by no more than 0.005 and log loss
  by no more than 0.01.

A projection-first challenger may alternatively qualify when validation
projection RMSE does not regress, latest projection RMSE improves, projection
RMSE improves in at least half of available rolling folds, validation side
accuracy regresses by no more than 2 points, latest and combined side accuracy
improve, at least one rolling side-accuracy fold improves, and the same proper
score safeguards hold. This non-inferiority path reflects the stated release
standard that a demonstrably better model is sufficient even if it is not
perfect; it was fixed before either r48 production helper was implemented.

Among qualifiers, the largest combined accuracy improvement wins; latest
accuracy is the first tiebreaker and latest log loss the second. These gates
were fixed before downstream selected-side action-policy results were
inspected. A market with no qualifier retains its production side selector.
Any cohort discovered by inspecting held-out results is labeled post hoc and
must additionally show chronological rolling support; its status may not be
misrepresented as a preregistered hypothesis.

## Price and downstream action gate

Raw outcome accuracy does not authorize a bet. A changed side must be joined
to its actual point-in-time locked price, with the original and opposite price
swapped coherently. Missing opposite price means that row is valid for raw
accuracy evaluation but ineligible for action evaluation.

The selected raw champion then enters the same predeclared margin, policy, and
price-scope tournament used by the r47 rebuild. A live actionable policy must:

- improve paired locked-price units in validation and not regress in the
  latest partition;
- improve after removal of the best date and across at least half of the
  rolling-origin folds;
- retain at least 75% of the incumbent board unless an independently validated
  promoted pool offsets demotions;
- report retained, demoted, promoted, and net board counts;
- avoid a material combined loss by side or predeclared odds band;
- preserve no-bet, hold, freshness, data-quality, exact-price, and correction
  safeguards.

There is no global `-120` or `-200` ceiling. Each candidate is evaluated against
the actual break-even probability of its offered price. A raw champion without
enough locked opposite-side prices may not manufacture an actionable bet or
inherit the old side's grade; it remains unreleasable unless a safe
non-flattening production treatment is proven from existing evidence.

## Release requirements

Every production behavior change receives new immutable identifiers, updates
`docs/current-model-releases.md`, and preserves old locked rows. Verification
requires the read-only chronological audit, focused runtime tests, exact
old-versus-new board replay, `npm run verify:model-change`, a current-slate dry
run, and a clean intentional commit containing only phase-owned files.
