# Player-prop source and execution repairs

The owner requested three independent reviews of evidence, mathematical and
grading behavior, and operational/cost correctness. Any confirmed correction
resets the clean-round count; two consecutive clean rounds are required. This
document identifies prospective source behavior and reproducible engineering
checks. Private production packets, receipts, financial and outcome reports
are excluded. A source-only audit is not proof of future prediction accuracy.

The initial reviews reproduced distinct defects in the existing pipeline:

- The native NBA writer fetched exact-game injuries and filtered Out players,
  but discarded the full fresh report before AI input construction. AI insertion
  could also recommend an Out actor even when its submitted source said Out.
- NBA/MLB live input construction could replace an original native availability
  header with the review cutoff, admitting a future source at an earlier time.
- Repeated feeds from one book could change the inferred market reference.
- The individual assessment bypassed the existing coverage certainty check.
- A qualified AI event could remain unpublished because the native row had
  selected another event in the same market. An AI-only scope could disappear
  when the first lock sweep's native candidate lacked that scope.
- A same-day schedule correction after an early review could disable full-month
  funding. The calendar cache could refuse an updated game without refreshing.
- The cleanup job could close a legitimately running review before its actual
  writer lease and bounded generation window ended.

R30 preserves the independent model as one input and retains the original joint
law mathematics. Typed own-player participation evidence qualifies action
separately from a conditional forecast. Nonparticipation is No Play; an explicit
later clearance restores the existing Over and Under paths. Unknown/unlisted
status and another player's injury do not imply health. Real provider update
dates take precedence over a later retrieval of an older status. The already
fetched complete NBA report, original normalized fields and actual response
completion are passed into the reader without another provider request.

Original native headers remain exact. Independently available NBA quote-only
actors may still be reviewed when a native head is unavailable. MLB identities
cannot be recovered from an unavailable future native mapping. Both sides have
later-cutoff/valid-header recovery controls. Assessments use the same existing
unsupported-certainty boundary as individual coverage. This lexical control
does not prove every future free-form inference is correct.

The inferred anchor counts normalized books rather than provider feeds. Equal
current conflicting observations cannot establish a vote. Original source
groups and prices remain inspectable; no money or informed-bettor identity is
inferred. Exact native declared event lines remain authoritative when supplied.

NBA publication retains a qualified exact native event when available and can
recover a genuinely current qualified alternative when it is not. Replaced
native event probabilities are not carried onto a different line. Observed
pre-lock sources, quote contracts, review authority, complete public metadata
and actual numeric readiness bind handoff frames. A local final completed before
lock need not disappear because a subsequent lookup crosses lock. AI-only scopes
require truthful pre-lock published evidence; withdrawal, changed context,
decline and unavailable authority remain distinct. Stored locked payloads win
before every new semantic and are never reconstructed or regraded.

V70 preserves started-slot identities, accepted results and usage. Current
pending clocks can reconcile without retroactively changing completed inputs.
The old RPC remains compatible, while revision checks and atomic calendar writes
refuse stale planners without poisoning newer funding. Targeted daily official
refreshes retain the full capture's age and are bounded. Cleanup respects the
exact active sport owner before lock, and abandoned unknown costs remain reserved.
Standard budget pricing, recovery, monthly caps and stronger quotas are unchanged.

Verification includes source/parser/grade/binding/lifecycle controls, paired
qualification and recovery, exact quote and probability equality, real
PostgreSQL old/new caller races and accounting, full model checks, clean latest
main integration, protected PR and actual production source plus normal review
and lock observations. All new test inputs and responses are synthetic/mocked;
there is no extra paid generation, count request, manual cron, replay or third
review. Same-law participation qualification leaves numeric projections and
probabilities exact. Publication recovery intentionally restores qualified AI
scopes rather than imposing a board-size or direction quota. Changes in actual
board coverage and spending require ordinary forward observation.
