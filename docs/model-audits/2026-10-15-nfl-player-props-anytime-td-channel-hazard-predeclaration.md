# NFL player props Anytime TD channel-hazard predeclaration

Date: 2026-10-15  
Status: frozen before execution; frozen Week 5 remains an external check

## Objective

Test whether separating touchdown mechanisms fixes the rejected pooled hierarchy while remaining
fully independent of sportsbook prices and probabilities.

## Frozen architecture

Predict three team scoring intensities separately:

1. receiving TD intensity generated by team pass TDs;
2. non-QB rushing TD intensity; and
3. QB rushing TD intensity.

For every channel, fit a regularized Poisson team model from strictly lagged team production,
opponent channel allowance, pace/state, and environment. Allocate each predicted channel only among
eligible players:

- pass TD: RB/FB/WR/TE using participation, conditional snap share, target share, red-zone and
  goal-line targets, and prior receiving TD rate;
- non-QB rush TD: RB/FB/WR/TE using participation, conditional snap share, rush share, red-zone and
  goal-line carries, and prior non-QB rush TD rate; and
- QB rush TD: QB using participation, conditional snap share, rush share, explicit scramble count,
  designed-run proxy, red-zone/goal-line carries, and prior QB rush TD rate.

Within each team/channel, non-negative eligible-player weights are normalized to the corresponding
team channel intensity. A player's hazards are summed and converted with
`P(at least one TD) = 1 - exp(-(lambda_pass + lambda_nonqb_rush + lambda_qb_rush))`.
Use the same preceding-season global Platt procedure as the fixed independent foundation.

Return and defensive TDs are not assigned to offensive player rows because the retained data lacks
a stable prelock role process for those events. The global preceding-season calibrator may absorb
their base-rate omission but cannot use market input. True routes remain unavailable; offensive
snap share and target share must retain their honest names.

## Prohibited inputs

Sportsbook line, price, market/no-vig probability, movement, spread, total, consensus, and the
released market residual are prohibited from team models, allocation models, and calibration.
Exact price and no-vig probability are downstream diagnostics only.

## Frozen evaluation gates

1. Select on 2024 against the fixed independent ensemble and confirm on untouched 2025.
2. Require lower Brier and log loss and no lower AUC in both seasons.
3. Require the 2025 game-clustered Brier-delta 95% interval below zero and every frozen
   chronological segment non-worse.
4. Report QB, RB, TE, and WR calibration/discrimination separately; no material hidden collapse.
5. Only after historical gates, report the frozen 45-row Week 5 ledger and require non-worse Brier,
   log loss, AUC, and calibration gap for release-candidate status.
6. Report exact-price positive-EV and top-5/10/20 rank economics for the independent probability
   and existing downstream market reader. Economics may not select or refit the model.

Production Anytime TD actionability remains disabled regardless of the result. Locked rows remain
immutable.
