# NFL player props release-pure loss-ledger predeclaration

Date: 2026-10-15
Status: frozen before loss/win component comparison

## Question

Across every settled canonical main-line scope retained in the 2026 immutable archive, which error
components distinguish independent-model losses from matched wins within each prop family, and
which market observations available by lock would have confirmed, cautioned, demoted, or corrected
the forecast without hindsight?

## Immutable evidence boundary

- Week 5 is the first complete full-board private lock archive. It contains every locked offered
  side and alternate, canonical-line flags, exact price, independent and published projections,
  probabilities, grade, releases, movement, and lock time.
- Weeks 1–4 retained only official actionable tracking rows. They remain a separately labeled
  actionable-history diagnostic and cannot be called complete-board evidence.
- The Week 5 market-observer archive contains provider opening, T-24, and T-60 rows when captured.
  Source classes and named books remain literal. Missing Pinnacle, NoVig, intermediate, or T-6
  observations are unavailable, never inferred from another book or a later week.
- Official current-season stats supply outcomes. Pregame feature values must be strictly shifted.
  Actual participation, team opportunity, share, and efficiency may classify a settled residual,
  but may not become a candidate input.
- Stored locked payloads are read-only and always take precedence. No projection, probability,
  grade, price, result, or evidence field may be reconstructed over a stored value.

## Family separation

The audit reports the eight supported families independently: Passing Attempts, Passing
Completions, Passing Yards, Rushing Attempts, Rushing Yards, Receptions, Receiving Yards, and
Anytime TD. It does not pool families to manufacture significance.

## Frozen root-cause components

For each settled loss and matched wins from the same market and position, report:

1. participation and role survival: actual participation/snap collapse versus shifted role prior;
2. team opportunity and game script: actual team pass, rush, target, or play budget versus its
   strictly shifted pregame expectation;
3. player share and workload: actual opportunity share versus shifted player share;
4. efficiency: realized yards/completion/touchdown conversion conditional on actual opportunity;
5. opponent and matchup: shifted position/opponent allowance and pressure/box/coverage features;
6. weather, injury, and availability: only pregame-known environment, availability, and depth;
7. distribution, tail, and calibration: point residual, distance from line, probability error,
   and tail concentration;
8. identity and data quality: unmatched outcome, missing position/feature identity, incomplete
   paired offer, or unavailable historical observation.

Every component must show losses and wins together. A component is repeatable only if its
loss-minus-win contrast is directionally consistent in the market/position cohort, has adequate
support, and is not merely an outcome-derived restatement of the loss.

## Market-reader classifications

At the exact canonical line, source-separated observations available by lock are classified as:

- `confirm`: qualified line/price direction supports the stored forecast;
- `caution`: qualified evidence is adverse but insufficient to establish an opposite side;
- `demote`: repeated/broad adverse evidence would remove an otherwise actionable grade while
  preserving the independent forecast;
- `correct`: only when target-excluded, source-qualified evidence supports the opposite side under
  a predeclared family-specific rule;
- `unavailable`: the necessary time/source/pair evidence was not captured.

The audit distinguishes line movement from price movement, named source and source class, breadth,
disagreement, and landmark timing. It also separates forecast-direction failure from exact-price
and grade failure.

## Advancement gates

No rule advances from this audit alone. A proposed independent candidate must use only pregame
features, improve chronological historical MAE and RMSE (or Brier and log loss for Anytime TD),
survive a later confirmation segment, and then improve the exact current archive without reducing
direction. A proposed decision rule must include paired promotion and demotion accounting, retain a
useful board, and validate outside the scopes that suggested it. Generic cross-family market rules
and automatic adverse-movement flips are prohibited.
