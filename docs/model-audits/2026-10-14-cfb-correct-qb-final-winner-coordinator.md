# CFB correct-QB final-winner coordinator — release audit

## Decision

Release the October 14 r2 coordinator for future unlocked College Football
predictions after all release gates pass. It supplements rather than replaces
the independent score model and the professional market reader. The incumbent
joint PMF remains the starting forecast; the coordinator may change the winner
only when its smooth, bounded, target-book-excluded combined probability crosses
the 50% boundary. A rebuilt coherent PMF then owns the winner, projected score,
Moneyline, Spread, probabilities, and downstream exact-price grades.

This is retrospective development evidence, not a guaranteed future hit rate.
Existing locked records retain their exact stored payload and release identity.

## Inputs and leakage controls

The frozen artifact uses preseason Elo and personnel priors, opponent-adjusted
rolling and recent-three form, EPA/play, true success, early-down efficiency,
pass/rush/QB efficiency, line yards, stuff/opportunity/explosive rates, red-zone
and third-down success, drives, yards per drive, field position, pace, rest, and
the incumbent reader margin. Market features keep Moneyline, Spread, total,
book coverage, chronology, and provider-separated split provenance distinct.

The exact evaluated sportsbook family is removed before market features are
formed. Named-sharp, fallback, and public splits remain distinct inputs; missing
fields use the artifact's frozen imputation plus explicit missingness indicators
and are not interpreted as support or resistance. The corrected QB target uses
the verified quarterback EPA-volume field rather than the defective aggregate
unit previously found in a draft artifact.

The artifact is
`cfb_final_winner_coordinator_artifact_2026_10_14_r2_correct_qb_target_excluded_smooth_net_accuracy`.
Its verifier reproduced all 348 validation rows with a maximum probability delta
of `3.885780586188048e-16` and all 19 winner-boundary crossings.

## Historical comparison

The selected candidate is `model_recent3_and_market_ablation`. On the exact
298-game complete-reader comparison, Moneyline improves from 232-66 to 235-63,
Spread from 101-79-3 to 102-78-3, Spread +3 from 121-62 to 122-61, and Spread +6
from 132-50-1 to 133-49-1. Margin MAE improves from 11.7589 to 11.6989. Totals
are unchanged because this release coordinates the final winner/margin, not the
total model.

On the broader 276-game forward comparison, ten interventions produce eight
Moneyline corrections and two harms: 197 correct becomes 203 correct. Spread
improves by two net wins, Spread +3 by two, and Spread +6 by one. The 77-game
Week 6 holdout has one intervention, one Moneyline correction, zero harms, flat
Spread direction, and improved margin MAE. The chosen candidate intentionally
accepts isolated harms when net prediction accuracy improves; it was not selected
for a zero-harm headline.

Using incumbent fallback outside the exact candidate subset, the complete
376-game released-record counterfactual is at least 286-90 Moneyline,
142-115-3 Spread, 166-94 Spread +3, and 185-74-1 Spread +6; totals remain
151-121. The fallback rows were not individually regenerated by the coordinator,
so these combined figures are labeled counterfactual rather than a separate
full-board replay.

## Current-board impact and missing-data behavior

A zero-write credential-backed rehearsal ran the sole production writer over 97
games. It produced 174 evaluated markets: 17 Best Angles, 45 Leans, 87
Watchlists, and 25 No Plays, with 62 actionables. Five winners changed, producing
three actionable promotions and two demotions; therefore the board does not
flatten. APP-CCU, CIN-WVU, BRY-Maine, Albany-Stony Brook, and Tulsa-Rice crossed
the winner boundary. Each changed projected score, winner, Moneyline, and Spread
remained internally coherent.

Missing prices still produce a projection but hold the wager. Missing split
feeds are modeled as missing, not opposition. An unavailable or ambiguous team
profile and any runtime exception return the incumbent forecast unchanged. A
target-book selection cycle also returns the incumbent result rather than
dropping a game or forcing No Play.

## Release and rollback contract

The production family is score/model/distribution/probability/calibration/decision
r25/r24/r22/r26/r25/r51; evidence/member/writer are r46/r63/r117; fixture,
outcome, compact snapshot, reader, and tracking are r89/r79/r49/r34/r47. The
market reader is r39 and the exact-price tuple is r38. The October 13 r50 decision
and October 12 r49 decision remain explicit immutable predecessors under their
stored r45/r62 envelope.

Publication requires the focused CFB suite, `npm run verify:model-change`, a
clean current-main integration-safety result, protected PR checks, and post-merge
verification of the live release IDs, shared `prediction_pipeline:cfb` lease,
writer health, provider coverage, board counts, score/side coherence, snapshot,
and unchanged locks. Roll back future unlocked computation to the October 13
r50 family on parity drift, mixed releases, board collapse, target-exclusion
failure, score/side contradiction, writer overlap, reader failure, or lock
mutation.
