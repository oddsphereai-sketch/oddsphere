## Predeclared repairs

1. A final probability strictly above 50% selects the first side and strictly
   below 50% selects the second. At exactly 50%, the already-authored
   independent side is retained. A market consequence that lands exactly on
   the contract may not manufacture a side flip merely because an ordered pair
   lists Over or Home first. Exact-price economics keep such a market
   non-actionable.
2. An incumbent Lean moves to Watchlist only when at least one named source
   opposes the final pick and that opposition is corroborated by a second
   opposing source, a distinct conflicting named source, or explicit
   `named_book_disagreement`. Isolated opposition, static context, held-risk,
   buyback risk without opposition, public pressure, and source counts alone
   cannot demote a Lean.
3. The paired positive rule remains r22's clean causal Lean-to-Best promotion:
   an applied score consequence must support the same final side, carry named
   support, and contain no opposition, conflict, buyback, held-risk, or
   unmatched named pressure. No new promotion threshold or board quota is added.

These are categorical evidence decisions, not weights, blends, votes, favorite
labels, score nudges, or outcome-fitted rules.
