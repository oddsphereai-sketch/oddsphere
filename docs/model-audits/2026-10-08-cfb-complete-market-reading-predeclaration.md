# CFB complete market-reading audit — predeclaration

Status: SELECT-only research. This audit cannot change a live projection, prediction, probability,
grade, stake, writer, cadence, provider hierarchy, member surface, lock or tracking record.

## Product question

Does the released CFB product combine its independent football forecast with pregame market
information in a way that improves Moneyline, Spread and Total predictions and coherent score-axis
error? A useful market read may confirm, adjust or reverse the independent forecast. It may not be
a generic market weight, an automatic consensus follow, a capped cosmetic nudge or a grade change
that leaves the projected score and prediction pointing in different directions.

## Evidence boundaries

- Evaluate CFB independently from every other sport.
- Use 2026 official final scores and only evidence observed strictly before kickoff.
- Keep every immutable prediction separated by its decision/model release and lock timestamp.
- Compare the football-only forecast, the authoritative post-market forecast and every
  counterfactual on the same game, market and line.
- Retained OddSphere evidence is the authority for what a released model actually knew.
- Paid The Odds API point-in-time observations are external historical reconstruction. They may
  validate a future capture or arbitration rule, but cannot be described as evidence consumed by an
  old lock and can never rewrite tracking.
- The completed Total reconstruction used 370 credits and left 19,355. A separately cached
  Moneyline/Spread reconstruction may use at most 740 additional credits, must stop if the provider
  reports a different per-snapshot cost and must preserve at least 18,000 credits.

## Frozen market-path questions

Each question is evaluated separately for Moneyline, Spread and Total where applicable.

1. Source leadership: did Pinnacle move first, did a second distinct book confirm later, and did
   the leader retain the direction through the final pregame observation?
2. Price before line: did paired no-vig price move at an unchanged line before the same source
   crossed to a new line, and did another source subsequently confirm it?
3. Timing and velocity: separate moves first observed more than six hours, within six hours and
   within two hours of kickoff. A late update is not treated as early information.
4. Resistance and buyback: distinguish a one-way move from an excursion that is materially bought
   back before kickoff. The final signal for a qualified buyback points with the resistance, not
   with the abandoned excursion.
5. Key boundaries: Spread crossings of 3, 7, 10, 14 or 17 and Total crossings of 41, 44, 47, 51
   or 55 are reported separately from moves that stay inside one band.
6. Originator agreement: Pinnacle is evaluated independently from the retail/offshore group.
   DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, BetRivers and BetOnline cannot masquerade as five independent sharp
   sources, and majority movement alone is not authorization to flip.
7. Split provenance: current complete Circa or another verified named sharp split is tested as the
   higher-trust channel. DraftKings/Playbook-style fallback evidence is tested separately and never
   relabeled or weighted as Circa. Public money and public tickets remain separate.
   Split availability is a categorical state: current named, current fallback, public-only, stale
   relative to the price/line observation, or absent. Absent is unknown rather than neutral.
   Chronology is tested explicitly: money-before-move with later market confirmation,
   move-before-money with later confirmation, contemporaneous agreement, money/ticket divergence,
   market resistance to money and market movement opposite the money. A split older than the move
   cannot corroborate the newer move.
8. Reverse movement: a move against a material ticket advantage is evaluated only when the source,
   price pair, line history and timestamps are valid. RLM is not assumed predictive.
9. Football context: quarterback/availability changes, weather, rest/travel and matchup inputs are
   used to explain whether a market move contains new information. Missing context cannot count as
   neutral corroboration.
10. Model disagreement: corrections are judged only where the market pattern disagrees with the
    independent or released side. Report corrections, harms, pushes, directional accuracy and the
    coherently reflected margin/Total error; a winning signal that never changes the model is not a
    model improvement.

## Chronology and gates

- Development: games through 2026-09-27.
- Confirmation: 2026-10-02 through 2026-10-04.
- Holdout: 2026-10-07 and later. The holdout remains too small until additional games settle; that
  limitation must be reported rather than hidden by blending releases or dates.
- A production candidate must improve corrections minus harms in both confirmation and holdout,
  not worsen the applicable margin/Total/team-score error, cover at least three dates, and beat the
  released authoritative forecast on the exact eligible population.
- The candidate must replay the entire board, report promotions and demotions, preserve a non-flat
  board, and regenerate one coherent score distribution, probabilities and sides before exact-price
  grading.
- If a candidate fails, the released behavior remains intact while its precise evidence continues
  to be captured. Failure never authorizes a weaker threshold or outcome-picked exception.

## Full-product continuation

After CFB is resolved, repeat this structure one model at a time for NFL, MLB, NHL, WNBA, NBA,
Premier League, Champions League, NFL player props and MLB player props. Each audit owns its sport's
sources, timing, market structure, matchup/news inputs, prediction categories and release gates.
There is no cross-sport market-reading coefficient.
