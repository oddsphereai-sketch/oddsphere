# NBA unmatched named-pressure guard

Owner direction: spot-check the live NBA reader as a sharp bettor, confirm that
every available sharp signal is interpreted, and repair any signal that is
captured but not applied to the final product. This is a product-semantics
correction, not a future-accuracy claim or historical outcome fit.

## Finding

The October 31 OKC-HOU board contained verified Circa Spread pressure toward
Houston with a 35 percentage-point money-minus-ticket gap. The exact Circa
quote immediately before that split observation was not available at the same
contract, so the reader correctly refused to call the subsequent quote held,
aligned, or opposed. It retained the split as `unmatched_quote_context`.

That fail-closed reaction classification was correct, but the final posture
discarded the named pressure entirely. OKC Spread could therefore remain Best
Angle without any conviction consequence from the opposing Circa evidence.
A sharp bettor cannot infer numerical score movement or a flip from an
unmatched split, but should not call the strongest recommendation fully clean
when verified named-book pressure directly opposes it.

An unmatched `sharp_adjacent_book` split directly opposing the final pick is
now retained as conviction risk. The existing grade policy can consequently
temper Best Angle to Lean. Agreement remains context-only and cannot promote or
confirm a pick. The rule does not alter the independent score, market score
consequence, side, probability, price, stake, or lock.

Actual same-book number/price chronology remains the only authority for score
movement and flips. A verified quote reaction continues to supersede the
unmatched classification when timing and contract identity are available.

Locked records retain exact stored payload precedence. The sole NBA writer,
schedule, provider requests, storage, and `prediction_pipeline:nba` lease are
unchanged. Focused regression coverage proves opposite named pressure tempers
only the strongest grade, aligned unmatched pressure does not promote, and no
score or side is fabricated.

The read-only production-input rehearsal covered all seven October 31 games and
21 markets. Nine locked markets retained stored payload precedence and were not

Spread moved from Best Angle to Lean because Circa's verified pressure opposed
the OKC pick. Actionable count remained 7 to 7, with zero actionable promotions
or demotions. Scores, picks, probabilities, prices, and locked records had zero
changes. The rehearsal made no provider calls or production writes.

A Best-to-Lean change remains actionable; any future loss of an actionable
