## Integration review and explicit policy limits

The current-level winner policy can replace an otherwise conflicting relative
margin path. That is the declared arbitration policy, not evidence that the
relative conflict was resolved. Such a replacement cannot authorize the new
price/handle promotion. Only an applied, nonconflicting relative episode can.
Static disagreement is never emitted as observed movement in the reader.

The sign-conditional policy has a discontinuity: with a fixed market margin
near -3 and independent margin just above zero, the candidate adopts the market
margin; just below zero, it retains the independent margin. A focused sensitivity
test deliberately exposes this jump. This predictive risk is not qualified by
the functional tests, same-board replay, or arithmetic coherence. No claim of
improved calibration, ROI or betting value is made.

An input hash change does not attest news consumption. Keep original anchors
across statistical revisions, record the changed key and `informationOverlap:
not_attested`, and rebuild from the new independent baseline without recursion.
For a legacy r3 baseline, reproduce the older clock using only genuine history
available six hours after each game's scheduled start; a mismatch cannot seed
an old anchor. New source arrivals retain their own real capture clocks.

Equal-source-time contradictory quotes quarantine that source/market from both
relative episodes and current-level arbitration. Invalid score consequences
