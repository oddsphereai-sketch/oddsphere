## Problem and resulting behavior

An ordinary production review could claim money with enough time for one wave
but insufficient time for its already budgeted correction. A valid HTTP response
with an invalid numerical draft then closed the game without sending correction.
The sole NBA writer also began props after long Daily Edge work; subsequent
ordinary invocations could end before their lifecycle closed while due prop slots
remained pending. These are process defects, independent of betting outcomes.

The shared reviewer and preparer reserve both complete phases before claiming.
The existing single-phase function stays unchanged for correction-phase checks.
Primary request timeouts reserve the correction, future waves and final handoff;
checks after acknowledgement/serialization use actual remaining time. A request
that never reached HTTP records a known zero and cannot buy its first response
under the name of correction. Known dispatched recovery and unknown-charge holds
keep their existing semantics. No validation rule is weakened to accept a draft.

Within the existing sport lease, NBA due props starts before independent Daily
Edge work and is awaited even if Daily Edge throws. Its source fetches share an
absolute preparation deadline derived from the remaining complete-phase reserve;
late non-due native lock processing does not reserve a hypothetical paid review.
The NFL products reuse their one refreshed current-season state and their same
leased handler; the prop promise is awaited before lease release on every exit.
MLB remains its existing sole prop route. NFL/MLB route ceilings are 540 seconds,
leases 600 seconds, and their explicit AI deadline is request start plus 480
seconds, including waits and source work. A new invocation cannot reset an old
invocation's elapsed time. Native forecasts, collection budgets and cadence stay
unchanged.

## Sparse numerical support repair

A separate fresh whole-product review reproduced an actual parser/publication
failure: with two genuine played values and a credible independent mean, an
extreme mean was accepted because the old guard required three observations.
Prior-only combined events exposed the same omission. This is a source-unit
validation defect, independent of whether the old pick won or lost.

The shared support helper now exposes the exact same mean admission boundaries
to admission and NBA named constraint cards/correction feedback. It reconciles
actual same-game observations, accepts canonical integer count/net-yard units,
refuses future played/prior anchors and conflicting component/opportunity
records, and derives combinations only from complete same-game components.
Fractional offensive/defensive rebounds cannot manufacture an integer total.
An exact combination prior never fabricates primitive support. Signed NFL
components need their own supported range before they can qualify a combined
forecast; otherwise extreme opposing values could cancel into a plausible total.

At least a real played span, a played value plus a credible differing exact prior,
or an existing qualified multi-game range establishes a mean boundary. Missing
support produces an explicit scope-level numeric-support decline. Repeated zero
rare events can use canonical plate appearances or a positive exact prior rather
than a false zero ceiling. Anytime-touchdown is a binary event with physical
mean units [0,1], not a cap on latent touchdown counts. No arbitrary multiplier,
prior-only copy, scenario-tail cap, probability clipping, grade flattening, new
source call or prediction calibration is introduced.

Positive controls include supported sparse means and source-head role changes,
Over and Under Best Angle promotions, an explicitly declined invalid scope
followed by supported recovery, signed negative yards, rare-event opportunity
support and integer pushes. Adversarial controls include zero/one/two histories,
combined-prior allocation, duplicate/conflicting/fractional/future inputs and
signed-component cancellation. Actual parser/member/lock controls preserve
original old laws and stored locks. Raw factual-source presence is separate from
whether those facts establish a credible numerical magnitude.

Frozen prospective board-impact replay is internal, with no historical law,
pick, grade or outcome mutation. It is an admission audit, not a counterfactual
win-rate claim. All-player review completion, qualified numerical scopes and
actual live priced rows remain three distinct coverage measures.

The fixed sparse fixture has two formerly accepted Best Angles; explicit decline
leaves one, while supported repaired Over and Under drafts each retain two, and
a genuine quantitative source-head recovery also retains two. These are paired
correctness controls, not a target board size or observed live-product impact.

## Terminal-wave spending guard

Fresh independent whole-product review reproduced a delayed claim-ACK boundary:
at the exact 420-second nine/sixteen-part admission limit, one millisecond of
claim delay prevented the first eight requests, but later waves still dispatched
before the whole review failed. This spent money on a now-unrecoverable partial
game. Root independently reproduced the actual-code virtual-time probe.

R35 rechecks the complete game reserve immediately after claim acknowledgement.
Each settled wave stops later dispatch when any mandatory part is unrecoverable,
or when a correction fails and no further phase exists. All already launched
siblings are awaited and their original receipts retained. Known zero unsent
requests remain distinct from genuinely unknown billed outcomes; no existing
hold is released. Successful parts and actors keep their existing retained path.
One normal logical slot finishes once; no paid proof or closed-job replay occurs.

Actual-reviewer tests cover 1/8/9/16-part delayed claims and queue boundaries,
exact timely acknowledgements, nine/sixteen-part unknown first-wave responses,
failed final correction waves, all sixteen timely primary/correction parts and
successful-part retention. Same accepted law/quote yields identical board values
and grades. This bounds avoidable spending; it cannot guarantee vendor response,
whole-slate completion, outcome accuracy or a future monthly total.
