## Favorite selection finding

In the single R36/R31 November 6–19 era, winner calls are 69–34 versus a book-
favorite benchmark of 75–28. The model chose 97 favorites (69–28) and six
underdogs (0–6). Mean probability on favorite calls is 71.53%, versus 71.13%
realized wins. This subgroup does not show an obvious blanket favorite-
confidence problem. Six underdog calls are too few to infer a reverse strategy
or a permanent ban. No quota, automatic inverse or favorite override is added.

There are 47 favorite-winner/underdog-spread combinations (winner 33–14, spread
25–22); only one has a dog line at most +1.5. Zero winner/margin or spread/
threshold incoherences were found. A predicted narrow favorite win can correctly
coexist with a slight dog-cover probability. A likely winner and an attractive
priced wager are evaluated separately by the existing price/grade policy.
