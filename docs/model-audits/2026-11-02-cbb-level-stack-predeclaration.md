## Estimator frozen before fitting

For each axis, define a fixed ordered expert pattern: independent coordinate
and one own-contract coordinate for each distinct physical reference book.
Fit nonnegative weights that sum to one by minimizing the mean squared error
of their combined prediction against the final score margin or Total.
Use the FULL UNCENTERED error second-moment matrix, including off-diagonal
dependence and bias. Do not substitute diagonal variances, outcome SD or a
guessed market percentage. No intercept, ridge, tuning grid, feature selection,
outcome-selected book priority, winner override or coefficient floor is added.

The finite-dimensional simplex optimum may be found by enumerating active
faces and solving the equality-constrained quadratic problem. Singular error
matrices do not automatically invalidate a uniquely identified constrained
optimum; multiple unresolved optimal future weight mixtures are unidentified
and cannot receive guessed coefficients. For two experts I and M, the result
must reproduce clip(sum((I-Y)*(I-M))/sum((I-M)^2),0,1). Identical experts have
identical in-sample predictions but do not identify a transferable unique mix.

This is a learned interpolation assumption, not a claim of having no weights.
A boundary optimum assigning zero independent weight is retained honestly;
no epsilon restores protected independent authority. Each source pattern owns
its coefficients. Missing-book renormalization or borrowing another pattern's
weights is prohibited. Unidentified/untrained axes retain independent means.

The estimand is a constrained minimum-MSE predictor of the final game outcome.
The common outcome-noise term c*11' adds the same constant under sum(weights)=1;
this does not estimate latent epistemic uncertainty or prove market truth.
Predictive error calibration remains a separate measured part of the law.

Method references, not CBB qualification:
J. M. Bates and C. W. J. Granger, “The Combination of Forecasts” (1969),
https://doi.org/10.1057/jors.1969.103; and the coordinated discussion of learned
predictive combination, https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.02030. Their results do not
qualify our particular source, horizon, sport or season-phase transfer.
