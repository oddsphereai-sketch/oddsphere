## Problem and behavior

A schema-valid scenario vector could attach the wrong meaning to a positional
coordinate or contradict its supplied statistical context while still passing
finite-number and physical-identity checks. A retained independent contract side
could also be mistaken for the most likely event, even when its probability was
below one half. A validation retry repeated the original request without the
actual finding. Correct arithmetic alone did not establish grounded forecasts.

R18 uses named arrays of canonical integer statistics with common scenario weights.
There is no residual-points conversion, touchdown-excluding completion conversion,
gained/lost yard conversion, or runs/RBI home-run double addition in new requests.
Explicit future prior reads compare actual probabilities; half-integer count events
permit an exact complement, while missing integer push/contract information stays
unknown. Canonical stat identities and combination derivation are unchanged.

New source admission compares a proposed mean with supplied played-game support
when at least three reconciled qualifying observations exist, expanded to include
all supplied independent means for that exact primitive or derived event.
MLB support joins the exact market-tagged individual game/start logs; it does not
confuse pitcher and batter strikeouts or outs and innings. Nested hit/total-base
contracts inherit a failing primitive scope. Repeated zero rare-event samples do
not create a zero ceiling, and integer-line push mass is not invented into an
impossible losing event. This is an admission
boundary, not a learned calibration, numerical clamp, or instruction to anchor on
the native mean. A genuinely changed role beyond that evidence remains unqualified
until a supported quantitative input is supplied. Sparse one/two-game samples do
not create a narrow mean bound. Entirely nonpositive yardage laws against positive
supplied production are checked separately. Conflicting duplicate provider values
are excluded rather than resolved arbitrarily. NBA DNP zero-minute records do not
become ordinary conditional production; offensive plus defensive rebounds are
summarized explicitly.

Combination checks also require both concordant and discordant scenario pairs
when at least three source games establish both patterns for the contributing
statistics. They preserve source-observed dependence rather than impose a fitted
correlation threshold. An unqualified pair affects only combinations containing
both primitives; valid standalone markets remain eligible.

The mean/support check is conservative and can flag legitimate future changes when
source inputs do not quantify them. It must not be described as a general proof of
forecast quality. Source gaps are explicit exceptions, with separate qualification
counts. Every requested player and market still receives an evaluation or explicit
exception. An exception cannot authorize publication of an invalid numerical law.
A later offered event outside supported tails is reported as unqualified; it cannot
acquire artificial certainty or cause a third review. Future event probabilities
remain estimates rather than empirically calibrated confidence.
