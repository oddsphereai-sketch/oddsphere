# NHL evidence-qualified Total package r22 predeclaration

Date: 2026-10-23

Status: predeclared, read-only replay. No live authority.

## Diagnosis

The universal non-negative exact-price rule is rejected: it removed Total
winners rather than ranking the lane. Historical NHL validation instead shows
that a model-to-line gap of at least 0.5 goal is the durable independent Total
band, while the current target-excluded multi-book reader improves the weak
2026 independent Total baseline. Total actionability should therefore require
one of those two forms of evidence rather than price edge alone.

## Fixed package

1. Use the fixed seven-game score-head handoff.
2. Use the frozen 2023-fit puck probability calibration in the seven-through-
   nine maximum-team-game window.
3. Keep an existing future-unlocked Total Best Angle or Lean actionable only if
   either:
   - the absolute independent Total projection-to-current-line gap is at least
     0.50 goal; or
   - the final model contains a target-excluded Total read for the exact line
     with at least two complete books, and the read-implied direction agrees
     with the final Total pick.
4. A failed Total becomes Watchlist. This Total rule does not promote a raw
   Watchlist; the calibrated puck lane supplies the package's paired promotion
   path.
5. Preserve sides, scores, margins, Totals, probabilities outside the frozen
   puck calibration, lines, prices, stakes, readers, writer, lease, and locked
   payloads.

## Gates

- Pre-r18 selection and combined r18/r20 confirmation overall actionable
  accuracy and exact-price units do not decline versus r20.
- r18 and r20 are reported separately and neither may lose more than one net
  actionable win versus r20.
- Combined-confirmation Moneyline, puck-line, and Total actionable accuracy do
  not decline versus r20 when the candidate retains at least ten rows in that
  market; smaller cells are reported but not used as proof.
- Full-cohort Moneyline and puck-line actionable units do not decline versus
  r20. Total accuracy does not decline and at least 15% of settled Total rows
  remain actionable.
- Full all-market actionable Brier does not worsen, actionable count does not
  exceed r20, and both promotions and demotions occur.
- The 2026-10-23 unlocked board retains at least one actionable, exact-price
  coverage is complete, and every locked row remains immutable.

Failure rejects the package. No Total gap, book-count, reader-direction, or
retention threshold may be retuned after results are opened.
