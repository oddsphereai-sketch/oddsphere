30-yard receiving game therefore has the same fitting influence as a 12-target game; the same issue
applies to catch rate, yards per carry, completion rate, and yards per attempt. Receiving Yards is
also fit directly as yards per target rather than as the coherent product of catch probability and
yards conditional on a reception.

The next candidate tests whether exposure-weighted, process-aligned efficiency heads improve the
