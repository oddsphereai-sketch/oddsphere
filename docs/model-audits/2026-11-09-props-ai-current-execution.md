Best Angle to Watchlist, while a genuinely improved latest price moves Watchlist
to Best Angle. Neither side is assigned a quota. Separate tests retain 60% push
mass on an ordinary integer Over while a same-number at-least milestone has its
own probability. NFL and MLB adapters agree on current price/EV/grade; MLB
promotion restores the standard stake. All preexisting locked rows are unchanged.
