# NBA Moneyline Best Angle evidence guard — predeclaration

Date: 2026-10-21

Status: predeclared candidate for future unlocked NBA rows only

## Problem

The active Moneyline grade path labels a positive model-minus-market probability gap as
`support`. That gap is model value relative to the current no-vig price; it is not proof that
the market moved toward the selected side. The professional market reader already keeps those
concepts separate, but its qualified observational Moneyline and sub-authority Spread reads do
not reach Moneyline grade evaluation.

On the October 21 board this lets CHA Moneyline qualify as a Best Angle even though the direct
Moneyline read is unavailable and the professional Spread reader has a persistent qualified
Brooklyn read. The Brooklyn evidence remains below the existing two-point score-correction
threshold, so it must not flip the winner or score. It is nevertheless contradictory evidence
that should prevent the strongest public grade.

## Fixed candidate

1. Preserve the independent-first score and every existing forecast-authority threshold.
2. Preserve direct Moneyline reads as observation-only: they cannot change a score, winner,
   probability, or price.
3. Preserve the existing two-point, multi-trail, named-book Spread correction as the only path
   by which the reader can change an NBA winner.
4. For Moneyline grading only, a qualified direct Moneyline read on the opposite side blocks
   `Best Angle`. When the direct read is unavailable, a qualified observational Spread read on
   the opposite side also blocks `Best Angle`.
5. The blocked row remains `Lean` when every existing high-quality, injury-availability,
   confidence, model-edge, and positive exact-price-EV gate passes. This is not a No Play cap.
6. A matching direct Moneyline read or a final side authored by the existing qualified Spread
   correction is explicit confirming evidence. Confirmation does not lower any probability,
   edge, price, quality, or injury gate and cannot manufacture a Best Angle.
7. Public ticket/handle splits remain context-only because source-specific NBA outcome
   validation is not available. They cannot flip, promote, or demote a play.
8. Member wording must distinguish model value versus the current price from genuine qualified
   market confirmation.
