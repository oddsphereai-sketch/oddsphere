## Explicit decision contract

The independent scorer authors baseline scores. Each exact same-book,
same-provider/run, two-sided paired contract is interpreted separately.
Number and de-vigged juice map to one coordinate under the released residual
CDF. The difference between causal endpoints is an observed economic
displacement, NOT a current-market forecast averaged with the model. The
selected episode applies that displacement once to the pristine independent
coordinate. Same-book ML+Spread can identify one location/scale together;
different books never synthesize a new mean, scale or probability.

Named source chronology determines an observed leader, followers, held
resistance, conflicts, reversals and frontier extensions. Equal-source-time
leadership is unidentified, not silently tie-broken by book preference. When
every simultaneous source yields the identical full coordinate (including
dispersion), that common effect applies once with `leader=null` and explicit
equivalent sources. Same direction or equal ML probability alone is not enough.
Copied followers add zero amplitude. An observed extension can replace the
prior frontier; successive distinct extensions are walked chronologically,
never the whole move added twice. Full buyback cancels its episode and cannot
promote a stale observed copy; unrelated opposite or different-amplitude moves
are not erased merely because they occurred within the cancellation window.
An opposite fresh peer remains unresolved; a genuinely later source episode
may supersede observations predating it. Partial buyback preserves the actual
net endpoint and tempers conviction for the recent contrary leg.

Every numeric change traces to the chosen physical source and its exact
endpoints. No percentage blend, Bayesian/logit posterior, median pooling,
raw-count vote, favorite quota or minimum-move activation is used. Necessary
identity, timing, freshness, numerical-identifiability and lock checks remain.
CDF conversion and solving a distribution are explicit mathematical
assumptions—not a claim that prediction can be free of mathematics.

A lone current quote is `context_only`: exact source/line/fair probability
versus independent probability, static agreement/opposition and executable
value. It is not fabricated movement or a pull toward the current line.
Static opposition can temper Best to a playable Lean; strong positive-value
independent forecasts can survive. Static agreement is alignment, not causal
sharp confirmation. Different quoted contracts are retained as such and do
not invent same-line opposition. Valid timed split reactions are classified
aligned/held/opposed with actual source types and quote-side direction; they
affect posture, not arbitrarily assigned points. Unresolved direction and
source-clock problems cannot become a false confirmation.
