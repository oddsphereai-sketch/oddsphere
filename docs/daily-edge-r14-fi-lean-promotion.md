# MLB Daily Edge r14 — additive first-inning promotion

Date: 2026-07-28

## Release identity

- Decision release: `mlb_daily_edge_decision_2026_07_28_r14`
- Public calibration: `mlb_public_calibration_v13_2026_07_28`
- Rule bundle: `mlb_daily_edge_rule_bundle_v15_2026_07_28`
- Grade policy: `mlb_public_grade_policy_v13_fi_lean_signed_edge_price_promotion_2026_07_28`
- New rule: `fi_lean_signed_edge_price_best_angle_promotion_v1_2026_07_28`

The probability heads, correction policy, tracking contract, prediction writer,
and sport-scoped `prediction_pipeline` lease are unchanged.

## Scope

This release preserves every existing moneyline, total, and first-inning rule.
It adds one promotion path:

- Start with an actionable first-inning `Lean` from the authoritative writer.
- Require final writer signed edge of at least 6 percentage points.
- Require current American odds better than -130.
- Preserve all existing no-bet/hold decisions.
- Promote the qualifying Lean to `Best Angle`.

This is a grade-only promotion. It does not change the predicted side,
probability, projection, stake, or number of actionable plays. It has no slate
count target, minimum, maximum, or cap. There is no new demotion.

## Locked current-head replay

The rule was evaluated on settled, locked first-inning records generated by the
active probability head and final writer semantics from 2026-07-11 through
2026-07-27.

| Window | Bets | Record | Profit | ROI |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Train | 5 | 3-2 | +1.115u | +22.3% |
| Validation | 15 | 11-4 | +7.051u | +47.0% |
| Untouched | 10 | 6-4 | +1.771u | +17.7% |
| Combined | 30 | 20-10 | +9.936u | +33.1% |

The 30 qualifying bets occurred on 12 dates. Combined leave-one-date-out ROI
remained positive, ranging from +27.7% to +40.2%.

The test uses the final writer edge stored at lock, not the similarly named
first-inning audit edge. Adding a confidence threshold reduced and destabilized
the validation cohort, so confidence is not used to force this promotion.

## Board impact and rejected changes

- Historical replay: `Best Angle` +30, `Lean` -30, actionable plays unchanged.
- The release does not attempt to produce a requested number of plays.
- Existing moneyline and total paths remain intact because no additional
  fixed rule passed all chronological windows.
- The total probability-distance clamp was not used as a volume lever. Removing
  it changed no historical side or ROI and produced unstable grade relabeling.
- Existing correction/flip rules are preserved. No unsupported automatic flip
  was added.
